BEFORE KICK-OFF

Good to know.

Clear answers for a more considered purchase.

Are these official KONAMI listings?+

No. Touchline Market is an independent storefront concept and is not affiliated with or endorsed by KONAMI. Account transfers may be restricted by the game's terms and can carry risks. Review the applicable rules before proceeding.

How do I contact the owner?+

Choose an account and tap its WhatsApp button. The message opens with the listing details filled in so you can ask the owner about availability and next steps.

Should I share my game password?+

Never share your password, verification codes, or recovery details in a message. Confirm the listing and transfer terms carefully, and review the game's rules before proceeding.

Does this website process payments?+

No. This website is a listing catalog. You contact the owner on WhatsApp to discuss the account and arrange any next steps directly.
